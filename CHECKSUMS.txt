Super Robot Wars L English v1.5

v1.5 adds the NDS12 dialogue font, pilot-screen and menu fixes, UI/unit-name polish, another name and story text pass, universal forward-compatible .bgmpak BGM packs, and a built-in Custom BGM Maker tab in the HTML patcher.

Two patches give the same patched ROM (hosted on GitHub):
- SRWL_English_v1.5.html: offline browser patcher (includes the built-in Custom BGM Maker tab), accepts both clean dumps below.
- SRWL_English_v1.5.bps: standard BPS patch for the canonical-header dump.
Both verified by patching fresh copies of the clean dumps; identical output hash below.

Optional universal vocal BGM packs (hosted on external mirrors; forward-compatible with clean JP, v1.3-v1.5, and future releases):
- SRWL_StereoBGM.bgmpak / SRWL_MonoBGM.bgmpak: apply inside SRWL_English_v1.5.html (or via tools/bgm_pack.py).
