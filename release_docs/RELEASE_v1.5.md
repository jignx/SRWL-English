## v1.5

Pilot-screen and menu fixes, plus another pass over names and story text. The music add-on is not part of this release yet.

### Screen fixes
- Pilot screens: Spirit and Skill names that came up blank (Intuition, Resupply, Attune, Disrupt, Combo Attack and others) now show in full, on the map pilot page, the Spirit list, the level-up screen and Upgrade Units.
- Stat and weapon lists now read "HIT" instead of "Hit".
- Menus: several labels that were blank or clipped now fit, including the Shortcut Settings labels, the X List overlap, the SP list Unit column and the Side: Ally row. The MAP Wpn and A.Combo battle tags fit their window.
- Rei Ayanami's name plate now reads Rei, and Rey Za Burrel's reads Rey.

### Names and text
- Chang Wufei is now in that order in the story text.
- Honorifics are gone from Ai's plate and 51 battle quotes.
- Dancouga Nova's attacks now use the official names (Dancouho, Final Dancouho, Dancou Dangaiken, Dancouken).
- Crow's Machine Gun is named correctly, and five lines that said Lou Liruri now say Roo Riruri.
- Three Choudenji lines now read Super Electromagnetic.
- About 20 typos and contractions fixed, plus a pass for consistent terms and names (Longo, Baleon, Borot, A.T. Field and others).
- Story lines break more smoothly: about 480 lines that left one word alone on the last row were rebalanced.

### Battle
- Safer text fitting in battle: long lines are checked more strictly, so quotes are less likely to cut off or glitch.

### How to update

- Re-patch from your Japanese ROM with `SRWL_English_v1.5.html` (or apply `SRWL_English_v1.5.bps` to the clean dump). A ROM patched with an earlier version can't be updated in place. Your saves keep working; back up your save and cold boot.
- **BGM add-on:** there is no v1.5 add-on yet. Earlier add-ons don't fit the v1.5 ROM.

### How to patch

Open `SRWL_English_v1.5.html` in your browser and pick your Japanese ROM, or apply `SRWL_English_v1.5.bps` to the clean dump. No earlier version needed. Saves from v1.1 and later carry over.

### Found a bug?

Please [open an issue](https://github.com/jignx/SRWL-English/issues) with the version, your emulator or hardware, where it happened, and a screenshot of both screens. A save near the problem helps. Please don't upload ROMs.
