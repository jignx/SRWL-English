## v1.3

A dialogue and presentation update, plus an optional vocal BGM add-on.

### What changed

- **Dialogue.** About 185 lines corrected for meaning and about 380 polished so conversations flow naturally.
- **Names.** Map locations and area titles show full names, and Mission Objectives name the pilot they need.
- **Fixes.** *Gundam Wing Endless Waltz* series title, consistent CIWS names, more room for button shortcuts in menus.
- **Vocal BGM add-on (optional).** Stereo, tested on 3DS, with a mono fallback.

### How to patch

Open `SRWL_English_v1.3.html` in your browser and pick your Japanese ROM, or apply `SRWL_English_v1.3.bps` to the clean dump. No earlier version needed. Back up your save and cold boot; saves from v1.1 and later carry over.

**BGM add-on:** in the HTML patcher, also pick `SRWL_English_v1.3_StereoBGM_addon.bps` (or `..._MonoBGM_addon.bps`) and it builds the BGM ROM in one go. It also works on an already-patched v1.3 ROM. Flips or ROM Patcher JS work too. The ROM grows to about 266 MB (stereo) or 197 MB (mono).

Mirror downloads for the BGM add-ons: [Gofile](https://gofile.io/d/ZFqUQLUS) · [1fichier](https://1fichier.com/?dr7bkbhgn2e4mplokv48) · [MEGA](https://mega.nz/file/iDg2yDrL#8hXJHM_HrCJRhk7IXASOVYhu0SVbWb0RWgQbSJf18lA)

### Found a bug?

Please [open an issue](https://github.com/jignx/SRWL-English/issues) with the version, your emulator or hardware, where it happened, and a screenshot of both screens. A save near the problem helps. Please don't upload ROMs.
