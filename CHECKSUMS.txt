Super Robot Wars L English v1.3.1

v1.3.1 fixes a freeze on the Saving screen with the vocal BGM add-ons. The main patch and the
patched ROM are the same as v1.3; only the two BGM add-ons changed.

Two patches give the same patched ROM:
- SRWL_English_v1.3.1.html: offline browser patcher, accepts both clean dumps below.
- SRWL_English_v1.3.1.bps: standard BPS patch for the canonical-header dump (same bytes as SRWL_English_v1.3.bps).
Both verified by patching fresh copies of the clean dumps; identical output hash below.

Optional vocal BGM add-ons apply to the patched ROM (use one). The HTML patcher
can apply one too, from the Japanese ROM or from an already-patched v1.3 / v1.3.1 ROM:
- SRWL_English_v1.3.1_StereoBGM_addon.bps: stereo vocal BGM.
- SRWL_English_v1.3.1_MonoBGM_addon.bps: smaller mono fallback.
